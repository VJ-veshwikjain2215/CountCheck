TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Your data in CountCheck.

CountCheck stores account details (name, email, business name, and a hashed password), product information, and count observations. Data is associated with your business workspace and used to provide the inventory counting service.

What gets stored in your browser?

A session cookie keeps you signed in. A pending count may be stored in your current tab's session storage so you can recover an interrupted save. It clears when the matching saved record is opened, or the tab's session ends. Sign out and close the tab on shared devices.

Count records

Counts are observations, not automatic stock corrections. They remain part of your workspace history. Avoid adding personal information or sensitive notes to item fields.

Operator and hosting

CountCheck is operated by Veshwik Jain. For support or data requests, contact Veshwik on LinkedIn. The website is hosted on Embarko and database records are stored in Neon PostgreSQL in Singapore. The service operator and hosting providers may have operational access; this is not end-to-end encrypted storage.

Retention and deletion

Workspace records remain until you delete the workspace in Settings. Deletion removes the account, product and count records from the live database. Neon's recovery history is currently configured for six hours; deleted data may remain in that history until its window expires. Provider operational logs follow their retention practices.

A separately scheduled backup service is not yet configured. Export count history when you need an independent copy. Availability and uninterrupted recovery are not guaranteed.

Account control

You can export count history, change your password, use your private recovery code to recover access, or delete the workspace through Settings. Keep your recovery code private. Updated 1 November 2026.
